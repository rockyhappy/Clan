## 🌗 2. Theme Modes Archetypes

### ☀️ Day Village (Light Mode)
- **Background**: Warm Medieval Parchment (`ClanParchment100`)
- **Surfaces**: Light Linen & Wood Containers
- **Typography**: Dark legible slate & earth tones (`ClanParchment900` / `#1C1B18`)
- **Accents**: Polished Gold and Forest Green

### 🌙 Night Base (Dark Mode)
- **Background**: Deep Obsidian & Slate (`ClanSlate950` / `#070B12`)
- **Surfaces**: Midnight Slate Panels (`ClanSlate900` / `#0D141F`)
- **Typography**: Parchment white & light sand (`ClanParchment100` / `#FFF8ED`)
- **Accents**: Luminous Gold, Elixir Pink, and Gem Emerald glows
